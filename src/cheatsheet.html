Labor & the economy

  • Knights (all workers) vs. AFL (skilled, Gompers) vs. CIO (industrial, 1930s) vs. IWW (radical)
  • Pro-labor: Clayton 1914, Wagner 1935, FLSA 1938. Anti: Taft-Hartley 1947, PATCO 1981
  • Panics/busts: 1873, 1893, 1929, 1973 oil, 2008
  • Free silver = inflation to help debtor farmers
  • Stagflation = inflation + unemployment (1970s)

Test-day tactics

  • About 120 questions in 90 min = 45 s each. Flag and move on.
  • There's no guessing penalty, so never leave a question blank.
  • Put a date on every option. Wrong answers are often from the wrong era.
  • Watch for EXCEPT and FIRST. Re-read the stem before you pick.
  • About 70% of questions are after 1914. Know the 1945–1975 period cold.
